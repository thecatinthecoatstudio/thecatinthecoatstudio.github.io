# The Cat in the Coat Studio

An independent software studio creating calm, privacy-friendly, offline-first applications.

## Philosophy

We build software that respects users.

Our applications are designed to work locally whenever possible, avoid unnecessary permissions, and never collect data without a clear reason.

## Projects

### StageLyrics

A teleprompter for musicians designed for rehearsals and live performances.

- Offline-first
- No subscriptions
- No advertising
- No cloud dependency

### My Little Victories

A calm place for your personal victories and meaningful moments.

Save small steps, warm memories, difficult days you made it through, and anything else that matters to you.

- Private and offline
- No account required
- No advertising or analytics
- No streaks, rankings, or productivity pressure
- Your data stays on your device
- Shareable victory cards
